Three Habits For A Healthier Business Cash Flow

Practical ideas to help make your financial routines a little more manageable.

BUSINESS / CASH FLOWKnow Where
You're Going.

Good financial routines rarely need to be complicated. Consistency, clarity and a willingness to ask questions go a long way.

Keep A Simple Forecast

A short rolling forecast can help you understand upcoming commitments. Start with the money you expect to receive, then note your regular and occasional expenses.

Make Invoicing A Routine

Consistent invoicing and clear payment terms help you understand what is outstanding. Set aside time each week to review payments and follow up where appropriate.

Review The Numbers Regularly

A small, regular review is often more useful than an occasional deep dive. Look for changes in sales, expenses and cash balances, then ask for advice when something is unclear.
