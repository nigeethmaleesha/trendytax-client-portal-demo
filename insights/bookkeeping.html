A Simpler Routine For Staying On Top Of Your Books

Practical ideas to help make your financial routines a little more manageable.

BOOKKEEPING / ROUTINESThe Simple Side
Of Staying Organised.
Keep records together✓
Review transactions✓
Stay on top of accounts✓

Good financial routines rarely need to be complicated. Consistency, clarity and a willingness to ask questions go a long way.

Keep Records In One Place

Choose a consistent process for collecting receipts, invoices and key business records. A simple system is easier to maintain.

Set A Weekly Check-In

Regular reviews help you catch missing transactions and reconcile records while they are still fresh.

Ask Questions Early

If an entry looks unusual, check it promptly. Accurate records help inform useful conversations with your accountant.
